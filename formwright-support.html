Getting started

What do I need to run Form Wright?

An iPad running iPadOS 18 or later. Apple Pencil is optional; every tool works with touch.

How do I make my first model?

Tap the blue plus button, then Cube. Drag the handles to move, turn and scale it. Switch to Points, Edges or Faces at the bottom to edit the shape, and use the Mesh menu for extrude, bevel, subdivide and the other operations. Quick help under Settings walks through the basics on the iPad.

How do I paint on a model?

Open Paint, select the object, then Fill it or pick a brush and paint straight onto the surface. Layers, texture size and the eraser live in the Paint panel.

How do I animate?

Open Animate. Save poses on the timeline, set keys on modifiers and objects, or use Perform to record movement live. Curves opens the graph editor for anything that has keys.

Projects and files

Where are my projects saved?

On your iPad, inside the app. Projects opens the gallery. Settings has Save a version, which keeps an editable snapshot you can return to.

Where do exports go?

Exports are written to the Files app under Form Wright, Exports. The share sheet can also send them to another app, AirDrop or Photos for videos.

Which formats can I export?

USDZ and GLB carry supported materials and animation. OBJ sharing includes its material and texture files in a ZIP. STL and 3MF are for 3D printing, with millimetre dimensions. Images and video render from the viewport.

Which formats can I import?

USDZ, GLB, OBJ and STL models, plus images for textures and video or audio for reference on the timeline. Very dense models are reduced on import so they stay editable.

Is anything backed up or synced?

Only through your own iPad settings. Form Wright has no account and no server. iCloud or iPadOS backups follow your device settings.

Camera, scanning and permissions

Why does the app ask for the camera?

Only for two optional features: handheld camera movement, which tracks the iPad as you move it, and room scanning on iPads with LiDAR. Denying access leaves every modeling tool working.

I do not see Scan room.

Room scanning appears only on iPads with supported LiDAR hardware, such as iPad Pro models. It is hidden on other iPads rather than shown as broken.

Why does Save Video ask for Photos access?

Choosing Save Video in the share sheet adds the rendered export to your photo library, which needs your permission. Save to Files does not.

Trouble

The app feels slow on a big scene.

Use Mesh, Reduce or Remesh on very dense objects, keep texture sizes at 1K or 2K, and close other apps. Lighter meshes paint and animate more smoothly.

Something crashed or lost work.

Reopen the app; the gallery and Save a version keep your latest saved state. Then email support with what you were doing. Diagnostic logs stay on your iPad and are only shared if you attach them.

How do I remove my data?

Delete projects in the gallery, manage exports in Files, and delete the app to remove its local data. Photos items, backups and copies you shared are managed separately.
