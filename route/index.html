How to copy the sheet from a photo
  1. Take a clear photo of the route sheet.
  2. Open it in Photos.
  3. Touch and hold any word on the paper — your iPhone highlights the text.
  4. Tap Select All, then Copy.
  5. Come back here and tap Paste & read.

Works one page at a time — paste page 2 right after page 1, the stops add up. Scrambled result? Try selecting just the address column, one line per address.
