FormWright 3D

3D modeling that belongs on iPad

One app to model a form, adjust its points, stack modifiers you can always undo, paint right on the surface, and export a file the rest of the world can open. Built for finger and Apple Pencil.

  • Polygon modeling with point, edge, and face editing
  • Texture painting, fills, and gradient maps on the model
  • USDZ, GLB, and STL export for AR, game engines, and 3D printing

Work with me

What happens when I join a team.

I once asked an AI to describe me as a protein in the human body, based on everything it knew about how I work. It said I'm a growth factor. Not the protein that maintains the system. The one that shows up and makes everything around it grow faster.

Most AI adoption stalls the same way. Teams buy the tools, run a few experiments, and go right back to how they always worked. I sit down next to people and we build something together, until someone says "wait, I just cut my review cycle in half."

Read the full storyShow less

I push past the status quo. If a workflow has existed for five years and nobody has questioned it, I'm questioning it. If there's a faster, smarter, more creative way to move, I'm prototyping it before the meeting ends.

And I never advise on something I'm not also doing myself. I ship my own apps, like FormWright 3D. I run new pipelines inside real productions at Asteria. I test everything on my own content, my own audience, and my own workflows first. This isn't theoretical for me. I genuinely love this stuff.

I spent years at DreamWorks Animation training artists. After that I went deep into AR, XR, and AI before most of the industry showed up. Underneath all of it, I'm a dad, a curious builder, and someone who believes we can make this future feel human.
