PuppetWright Privacy Policy

Updated October 25, 2026

Everything happens on your device. PuppetWright has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing you film, scan or import is sent to us.

About this policy

PuppetWright is an augmented reality character app by Don Allen Stevenson III. This policy explains what PuppetWright uses on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Vision Pro, why, and what happens to it. It covers the App Store and TestFlight versions of the app.

On-device by design

PuppetWright does not connect to the internet for any of its features. There is no PuppetWright account, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Everything PuppetWright does with your camera, room scans, recordings and files happens on your device. We do not receive, sell or share your data, because it never leaves your device unless you share it yourself.

Camera and AR

PuppetWright uses the back camera with Apple's ARKit to understand your room, find the floor and place your creature in it. The camera picture is shown on screen as it happens and is saved only when you tap Record. People Occlusion lets people in front of the camera hide the creature; ARKit works this out live for each frame, and nothing about the people in view is stored.

LiDAR room scanning and 3D scenes

On devices with LiDAR, ARKit builds a 3D mesh of nearby surfaces (floors, walls, furniture) so your creature can walk on them and stop at walls. The scan stays in memory while you use the app and is discarded when you close it or tap Clear Scan. If Save 3D Scene with Recordings is on, each recording also saves a 3D scene file (USDZ) in PuppetWright's folder in the Files app. It contains the camera's path, the creature's animation and, if Include Room Scan is on, the room mesh. These files stay on your device unless you share them. Both options are in Settings > Recording.

Front camera and your face position

In front camera mode, PuppetWright uses ARKit face tracking with the TrueDepth camera for two things: so your creature can turn its head to look at you, and so your head can hide the creature when it walks behind you. PuppetWright reads only the position and direction of your head. It does not read facial expressions or face shape, and it does not identify you. This information is used live, in memory, and discarded frame by frame. It is never saved, never sent anywhere, never shared and never used for advertising or profiling. People Occlusion on the front camera works the same way as on the back camera. Videos you record with the front camera contain the camera picture, as any video does; they are saved only when you tap Record.

Apple Vision Pro

On Apple Vision Pro, PuppetWright uses visionOS world sensing: the floor and other flat surfaces, and a map of the room's shape, so your creature can stand on your floor, stop at walls and hide behind furniture. It also uses where your head is, so the creature can look at you and move relative to where you face. Vision Pro gives the app no camera picture, no view of your eyes, and no hand data beyond the system's look-and-pinch. All of this is used live, in memory, and discarded as it updates. It is never sent anywhere, never shared and never used for advertising or profiling. If you save a take, the 3D scene file contains the path your head took and, if Include Room Scan is on, the room map; it stays on your Vision Pro in the Files app unless you share it. PuppetWright's Record button makes its video inside the app: while you record, it notes where your head is and how the creature moves, then draws the creature and a faint outline of the room from your point of view and saves that video to your Photos. It contains no camera picture. You can also film with Vision Pro's own Record My View, which saves to your Photos under Apple's control. If you choose a frame (16:9, 9:16 or 1:1) and allow Photos access, PuppetWright looks for the new Record My View clips you make while it is open, saves a copy cropped to that frame to Photos, and shows it in a small preview. It does not look at your other photos or videos, the original clip stays as it is, and nothing is uploaded.

Recordings and Photos

When you tap Record, PuppetWright asks for microphone permission and records your voice and the sound in your room together with the video. Recording starts only after you allow access. The microphone stops when you stop the take, leave the app, or audio capture is interrupted. Temporary audio files are used on your device to synchronize sound with the video and are removed after the take is processed. Videos with audio are saved to Photos with your permission. On iPhone and iPad the app requests add-only Photos access, so it cannot read your existing library. On Vision Pro, Photos access also supports framing new Record My View clips as described above. Record My View uses the system's own microphone controls. No recordings or microphone audio are sent to us.

Imported characters and your files

If you import a character, the file you pick in Files is copied into PuppetWright's own folder on your device and read only to show and animate that character. Imported files are never uploaded. You can remove them in Settings > Imported Characters or in the Files app.

Game controllers

PuppetWright works with game controllers through Apple's GameController framework. It reads the buttons and sticks to move your creature, may set the controller's light color, and shows the controller's name and battery in Settings. None of this is stored or sent anywhere.

Settings stored on your device

PuppetWright remembers your choices, such as Cool Running and the recording options, in its settings on your device so they are kept between launches.

Permissions, deletion and your choices

Camera access (world sensing on Vision Pro) is needed for AR. Microphone access is needed to record videos with room audio. Photos access is used to save recordings and, on Vision Pro, frame new Record My View clips. You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app under PuppetWright. To delete what PuppetWright made: remove recordings in Photos, and remove 3D scenes and imported characters in the Files app (On My iPhone, On My iPad or On My Vision Pro > PuppetWright) or in PuppetWright's Settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. Copies you have shared or that are in your device backups are managed outside PuppetWright.

Apple purchases and TestFlight

Apple handles App Store purchases and payments under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details; Apple may give us standard sales reports. If you test PuppetWright through TestFlight, Apple handles invitations and installation, and any crash reports or feedback you choose to send, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children's privacy

PuppetWright does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account, no ads and no chat.

Support and feedback

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to reply and fix problems. Include only what you are comfortable sharing.

This website

This policy describes the PuppetWright app. Like any website, loading this page sends ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser type, to the site's hosting provider, Cloudflare, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. This page does not use analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Changes and contact

If PuppetWright's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. The latest version is always at donalleniii.me/puppetwright-privacy. Questions: donallenthethird@gmail.com.
