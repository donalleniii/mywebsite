PuppetWright Support

Questions, help and contact

Need help? Email donallenthethird@gmail.com.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later that supports ARKit. Devices with LiDAR (iPad Pro and iPhone Pro models) also scan your room, so creatures stop at walls and walk behind furniture. Front camera mode needs a device with a TrueDepth camera.

Do I need an account?

No. PuppetWright has no account and nothing to sign in to. Everything happens on your device.

How do I place and move my creature?

Move your device slowly so PuppetWright can find the floor, then tap to place a creature. The left stick moves it, the right stick turns it, and the Look button aims its head. Tap the floor again to move it. Pick a different creature from the menu at the top.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. Any game controller supported by iPhone or iPad works. Put the controller in pairing mode, then choose it in Settings > Bluetooth, or connect it with a cable. PuppetWright finds it automatically; Settings > Game Controller shows the controls.

How do I record?

Tap the record button. The video (picture only, no sound) is saved to your photo library. Choose the framing before you record, and use Hide to keep the controls off screen. Recordings never include the on-screen controls.

What are 3D scenes?

With Save 3D Scene with Recordings on, each recording also saves a USDZ file with the camera's path and the creature's animation, and the room scan if Include Room Scan is on. Find them in Files > On My iPhone (or iPad) > PuppetWright > Scenes, ready for 3D apps such as Blender or Reality Composer.

What is front camera mode?

Front camera mode puts you in the shot with the creature. Scan the floor with the back camera first, tap Flip, and hold still for a moment. The creature can look at you, and your body hides it when it walks behind you. Your face position is used live and never saved.

Can I bring my own character?

Yes. Import Character takes a rigged USDZ file from Files. PuppetWright finds the legs, head and tail, turns the file's animations into buttons, and walks the character itself if the file has no walk. Imported files stay on your device.

Can I use what I make with PuppetWright?

Videos and scenes you record are yours. The bear character is based on a model by Nyilonelycompany licensed under CC BY 4.0; if you publish footage of the bear, please credit "Bear model by Nyilonelycompany (CC BY 4.0)". See Credits and licenses.

How do I delete my data?

Remove recordings in Photos, and remove 3D scenes and imported characters in the Files app or in PuppetWright's Settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

How do I get help?

Email donallenthethird@gmail.com. Include your device model and what you were doing; screenshots help.
