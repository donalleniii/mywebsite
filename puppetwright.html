The idea

You're the puppeteer and the camera.

Most AR creatures are stickers. They float in the middle of the shot, play one loop, and never feel like they're in the room with you.

PuppetWright is the opposite. Each creature has weight and follow-through. It walks on your actual floor, stops at your actual walls, and does what you tell it to, the moment you tell it. Left thumb moves it, right thumb turns it, the buttons trigger its moves, and your phone records the take.

It's puppetry for the camera in your pocket. Point, perform, and walk away with a take that has real timing, real blocking and a real room behind it.

From take to finished shot

Perform it here. Style it anywhere.

PuppetWright records your performance as a rainbow skeleton moving through your real room: every step, turn and move exactly where you put it.

That take is a motion guide. Feed it to an AI video style-transfer app and the skeleton becomes a bear in your office or a crab in the fog.

The realistic versions were made in a separate AI video app, not in PuppetWright. PuppetWright gives you the performance; you choose the look.

What it does

Place it. Play it. Film it.

Seven creatures, a game-style controller, and a camera that knows where your floor and walls are.

Seven creatures

A bear, cat, raptor, horse, whale, eagle and crab, each animated with weight, follow-through and natural motion.

Drive it like a game

Touch sticks or a game controller. Walk, run, turn and stop, plus dozens of actions: roar, pounce, rear, breach, snap.

It knows your room

On LiDAR devices, a room scan lets creatures walk on your floor, stop at walls and hide behind furniture. Other devices use floor detection.

Get in the shot

Flip to the front camera and perform next to your creature. It turns its head toward you.

Clean takes, plus 3D

Record clean video straight to Photos. Each take also saves a 3D scene you can open in your 3D tools.

Bring your own characters

Import a rigged USDZ character and puppet it the same way.

Also on Apple Vision Pro

Your creature in your room, life size. Look and pinch to place it and send it around, or pick up a game controller.

How it works

Three steps to your first take.

  1. 01

    Find the floor

    Move your phone slowly until a ring appears on the floor. On LiDAR devices the room is scanned as you go.

  2. 02

    Place and play

    Tap to drop a creature. Left stick moves, right stick turns, and the action buttons trigger its moves.

  3. 03

    Record the take

    Hit record and perform. The video lands in Photos and the 3D scene in Files.

Questions

Good to know.

When does it come out?

As soon as Apple finishes its review. Join the waitlist and you'll hear the same day.

Does PuppetWright make the realistic creature videos?

No. PuppetWright records the performance: a rainbow skeleton moving through your real room, plus a 3D scene of the take. The realistic bear and crab in the clips above came from running those takes through a separate AI video style-transfer app. PuppetWright doesn't include or connect to one; you bring your own.

What devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 26 or later.

Do I need LiDAR?

No. LiDAR (on Pro iPhones and iPad Pro) adds the room scan, so creatures stop at walls and hide behind furniture. Other supported devices find your floor and work without it.

Does the app need an account?

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Everything happens on your device. Read the privacy policy.
