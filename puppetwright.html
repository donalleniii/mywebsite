Play a creature in your room

An AR performance app by Don Allen Stevenson III

Puppeteer a bear, cat, raptor, whale, eagle, crab or horse in augmented reality while you film. Everything happens on your device.

What it does

  • Seven animated creatures with weight, follow-through and natural motion.
  • Touch sticks or a game controller, with walk, run, turns and dozens of actions.
  • LiDAR room scanning so creatures walk on your floor and stop at walls.
  • Front camera mode to put yourself in the shot.
  • Record clean video, and save 3D scenes of each take for your 3D tools.
  • Bring your own rigged characters.
  • On Apple Vision Pro: your creature in your room, with look and pinch or a game controller.
