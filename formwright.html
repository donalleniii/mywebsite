The goal

"There just isn't an iPad app that can…"

The iPad has the chip, the display, and the best drawing input ever put in a tablet. But every time I wanted to make a 3D model on it, I ran into the same wall. Sculpting apps that can't do clean polygon work. Modelers with no way to paint. Painters with no way to model. And every workflow ends with "now send it to a desktop."

FormWright exists to remove that sentence. The goal is one app where you can start from a blank scene, build the form, adjust the points, add modifiers you can always undo, paint on the surface, and walk out with a file the rest of the world can open. Without ever leaving the iPad.

Not a desktop app squeezed onto a touchscreen. A modeling tool designed for the way your hands actually work on glass.

What it does

Make. Move. Shoot.

Everything below happens in the same project, on the same canvas, with your finger and an optional Pencil. Creative processing stays on your device.

Make

Polygon modeling

Points, edges, faces, modifiers, instances and symmetry. Clean topology you can take anywhere, not a sculpted blob.

Make

Sculpt & Branch Forms

Push clay spheres around with brushes, or grow connected organic forms and preview the mesh before you commit.

Make

Solid parts & ring blanks

Sketch, extrude, cut, fillet and chamfer with exact dimensions. Export STEP, or bake a mesh to sculpt and paint.

Make

Paint & live materials

Paint straight onto the surface with texture layers and symmetry. Live procedural materials, no UV round trip.

Move

Auto Rig & animation

Guided Auto Rig and Face Auto Rig, then keys, curves, ghosts and trails. Record Perform takes and layer them in.

Move

Physics to keys

Preview supported rigid-body reactions, then bake them into keys you can still edit.

Shoot

Light, camera, action

Lights, shadows and photo-based environments. Frame camera takes, add audio or your voice, and export video.

Shoot

Motion capture & AR

On supported hardware: handheld camera control, mixed reality, and body, hand or face capture.

Files

Real exports

USDZ, GLB, OBJ and FBX for scenes. STL and 3MF for printing. STEP for exact Solid bodies.

How it's made

Built by one person, for one device.

I'm Don Allen III. I've spent years advising companies on spatial computing and AI, and shipping my own creative tools on the side. FormWright is the one I kept wishing someone else would build.

Native to iPad and iPhone

Full-screen controls on iPad, compact panels on iPhone, GPU rendering on both. Scene complexity, texture size and device hardware affect performance.

Touch first, Pencil precise

Every tool was designed for a finger first, then refined for the Pencil. Pencil is optional, and nothing depends on a keyboard, a mouse, or a right-click.

Shipped in the open

Builds go to real testers through TestFlight, and the feedback shapes the next build. What you see on Instagram is the actual app, not a render. Join the beta.

Local, private, no lock-in

No account, ads or tracking SDKs. Projects save locally, and standard formats carry supported geometry, materials and animation out. Each format carries a different subset of your scene.
